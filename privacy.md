# Bumblebee Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 24 November 2026*

Bumblebee is an AI support assistant (a Discord bot) operated by Pollen Robotics on the Pollen Robotics Discord server. This policy explains what data Bumblebee processes, why, and what you can do about it.

## Who is responsible

Pollen Robotics SAS, 2 Place Jean Jaurès, 33000 Bordeaux, France, is the data controller.
Contact: [contact@pollen-robotics.com](mailto:contact@pollen-robotics.com)

## What data Bumblebee processes

Bumblebee only processes:

- messages posted in the support channels where it operates,
- messages posted in the threads it answers in,
- messages that mention it.

For these messages it uses your Discord user ID, your display name, the text of your message and the file names of any attachments (not their contents).

Discord delivers to the bot other messages from channels it can see. Bumblebee discards them immediately: they are never stored, logged or sent anywhere. Bumblebee does not read direct messages, does not track you across servers and does not build profiles.

## Why

Only to answer your support questions. The legal basis is Pollen Robotics' legitimate interest in providing support to its community (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)).

## Who your data is shared with

- **Anthropic, PBC** (the Claude API) receives the messages listed above to generate Bumblebee's answers. Anthropic processes this data as a service provider under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to train its models on this data. See [Anthropic's Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy).
- **Discord** hosts the messages themselves, under [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy). Bumblebee's answers are ordinary Discord messages.

Your data is never sold or used for advertising.

## How long it is kept

- **Conversation context** (the messages of a support thread, kept so Bumblebee can follow the conversation): kept **in memory only** on a server operated by Pollen Robotics in France, never written to disk, and **deleted automatically at most 30 days after the conversation started** (or earlier, when the server restarts).
- **Opt-out list** (Discord user IDs of people who opted out): kept until you opt back in.
- **Technical logs**: contain no message text, only metadata such as timestamps and processing costs.

Messages posted on Discord stay on Discord until you or the moderators delete them.

## Your choices and rights

- **Opt out at any time:** use the `/optout` command on the server. From then on Bumblebee ignores your messages entirely: they are not processed, stored or sent to Anthropic. Use `/optin` to undo it.
- **Or simply don't** post in the support channels or mention Bumblebee.
- Under the GDPR you can request **access to, correction or deletion of** your data, or object to its processing, by emailing [contact@pollen-robotics.com](mailto:contact@pollen-robotics.com) with your Discord username. We answer within 30 days.
- You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr).

## Changes

We may update this policy when Bumblebee changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
